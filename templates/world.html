THE CRAFT & THE JOURNEY

World of Earl

A licensed Interior Designer turned visionary fashion artist, Earl Gariando’s journey is defined by a relentless pursuit of excellence in Filipino craftsmanship.

A Foundation in Design

Graduating from Eastern Visayas State University in 2004, Earl built his early career in visual merchandising before translating his eye for display into couture fashion.

Acclaimed Recognition

His work has graced the finals of the most prestigious competitions in the Philippines, including the Philippine Fashion Designers Competition (2007) and Mega Magazine’s Young Designers' Competition (2009).

Global Excellence

In 2013, Earl was recognized on the global stage as a winner in the Look of the Style Awards and a recipient of the Sheffield Hallam University Scholarship in the UK.

Present Day

Today, Earl Gariando serves as a ManilaWear Designer at ManilaFAME, maintaining a steady collection of couture accessories under his label at FIRMA, Greenbelt 3.
